Who We Are
About Ntiyaya Trading

Ntiyaya Trading is a South African multi-disciplinary service company providing integrated solutions across mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, construction, quantity surveying and project management.

We are built on the belief that professional excellence and client-focused delivery are not mutually exclusive. Our team brings together qualified engineers, surveyors and project managers who work collaboratively to deliver measurable value on every project.

From small-scale installations to large infrastructure programmes, Ntiyaya Trading applies the same commitment to quality, safety and innovation. We pride ourselves on building lasting relationships with our clients through trust, transparency and consistent delivery.
